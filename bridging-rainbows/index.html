Kizuna impact projection

Planning model for one Delhi operating cluster starting from roughly 2,000 mapped street dogs.

Assumptions shown
6 MONTHS

Make the invisible visible

  • 2,000-2,500 dogs mapped with Kizuna IDs
  • 60-65% rabies vaccination status captured
  • Pickup and release proof attached for 1,000+ ABC journeys
  • Priority rabies and bite cases visible within hours
12 MONTHS

Reach protective coverage

  • 3,500-4,000 dog records across adjacent wards
  • 70%+ vaccination coverage target in active wards
  • 2,000+ sterilisation records with batch and vet history
  • 25-35% reduction in repeat unmanaged complaint hotspots
2 YEARS

Stabilise the ABC chain

  • 8,000+ dogs mapped across a larger Delhi cluster
  • Booster reminders reduce lapsed rabies protection
  • 40-50% faster pickup-to-release cycle for routine ABC cases
  • Evidence-led ward planning replaces ad hoc drives
5 YEARS

Build a city playbook

  • 25,000+ longitudinal street dog records
  • Sustained 70%+ dog vaccination coverage in managed wards
  • Fewer puppies entering unmanaged street populations
  • Replicable civic model for other NGOs and municipalities

Model assumptions

These numbers are not claimed outcomes. They are operating targets based on what Kizuna can coordinate.

1
Starting cohortOne Delhi cluster with around 2,000 street dogs actively mapped.
2
Core leversDigital ID, rabies vaccination status, ABC scheduling, release proof, and bite/rabies escalation.
3
Impact logicReliable field data improves coverage, reduces missed follow-ups, and makes public-health action faster.

Expansion roadmap

The wider ABCD programme layer.

Field workflow comes first. These modules can be added as BRF brings more civic, AWO, and public stakeholders onto the platform.

1

Government circulars

ABC rules, circulars and SOP material in English, Hindi, and local languages.

2

AWO registry

Training status, tender eligibility, achievements, complaints and performance history.

3

Community education

Awareness material, KAP questionnaire, downloads, and co-existence education.

4

Dog bite pathway

Evidence capture, fourteen-day observation, objective assessment and humane education.

5

Van and release tracking

GPS-tracked vans, original-location drop-back, release proof and resistance escalation.

6

Post-release feedback

Public reporting for missing returns, post-release illness, pregnancy after sterilisation, or rabies concern.
