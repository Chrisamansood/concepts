Live ABC journey

Each card is a working state in Kizuna. A coordinator can open any dog, inspect evidence, move the case forward and close the loop with release proof.

Report to release
1
Report

Citizen or feeder submits photo, GPS, landmark, sex if known, and case type: ABC, suspected rabies, bite case or post-release concern.

2
Match

Coordinator checks whether the dog already has a Kizuna ID. Possible matches require human confirmation before histories merge.

3
Schedule

System proposes pickup window, assigns field queue and enables citizen reminder to be present during catching where possible.

4
Catch

Field team tags the dog on site with timestamped image, precise private GPS and pickup handoff note.

5
Treat

ABC centre logs surgery, rabies vaccine, vaccine batch, cold-chain check, recovery status and daily post-op note.

6
Release

Van takes the dog back to the original location, records photo proof and flags resistance or safety issues.

7
Feedback

Community confirms return, reports post-release illness/death, pregnancy after sterilisation, or rabies concern.
