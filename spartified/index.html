Why Spartified?

Spartified is already close to high-value telecom workflows. The missing piece is a product layer that turns those workflows into measurable revenue, assurance and action.

Revenue Signals Are Scattered

Roaming rates, eSIM performance, partner reports and fraud exposure sit in different operational views. The agent brings them into one commercial story.

Visibility Without Action Is Weak

Dashboards show symptoms. Operators still need someone to explain the cause, rank the next step and prepare the action for review.

Connectivity Needs A Margin Brain

Connectivity is often sold like a commodity. Agents can point to the specific partner, route, segment or offer where the economics can improve.

Product Agents: Built Around The Portfolio

Roaming Margin Agent: finds partner routes, destinations and wholesale rates that need commercial attention.

Travel eSIM Offer Agent: drafts bundles for direct, reseller, retail and corporate channels using usage and conversion signals.

Fraud Intelligence Agent: explains suspicious patterns, simulates rule impact and prepares safe review actions for FraudProtect.

How The Product Runs

Keep the platform practical. Use a proven orchestration stack, give every agent a tight job, and log every decision path.

01

Telecom Connectors

IR.21, RAEX, TAP/CDR, eSIM lifecycle, fraud database, partner rates, API usage and ticket history.

02

LangGraph Orchestration

Stateful workflows for routing, tool calls, retries, approvals and agent handoffs.

03

LangSmith Observability

Trace prompts, retrieved data, tool calls, latency, cost, confidence and decision path.

04

MCP-Style Tool Layer

Expose Spartified product capabilities as controlled tools that agents can call with clear permissions.

05

Validators & Guardrails

PII handling, policy checks, hallucination checks, confidence thresholds and spend circuit breakers.

06

Channels

Operator portal, partner portal, API, WhatsApp, Telegram and voice depending on the customer workflow.
