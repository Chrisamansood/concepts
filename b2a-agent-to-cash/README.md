# B2A: Business to Agent — Agent-to-Cash

This is a concept prototype showing how a telecom commercial platform might govern and rate purchases made by enterprise agents, consolidate charges, and prepare supplier settlement while payment rails move funds.

## Files

- `index.html` — interactive transaction replay and enterprise console.
- `call-flow.html` — sequence diagram for the five-cent weather purchase and two caught failures.
- `call-flow.png` — static export of that diagram.

## Scenario boundaries

Kestrel Logistics, SkyCast Weather, PortSignal, agent IDs, invoices, transaction volumes and prices are fictional or illustrative. The prototype demonstrates a proposed flow; it does not represent a live operator integration, customer deployment, market price, payment service, or verified business case. The €0.005 operator allocation follows the example's assumed 10% fee on a €0.05 purchase. Real commercial terms, fulfilment evidence, regulated payment roles, privacy requirements and jurisdictional obligations would need to be agreed and assessed.

The payment networks and payment service providers remain the money-movement layer in this design. The proposed BSS role covers mandates, rating, billing, reconciliation and partner allocation around that payment flow.

The companion blog was used as concept source material and remains in the author's local research files.
